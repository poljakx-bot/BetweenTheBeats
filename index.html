A literary trilogy with an open physics companion

The Logical Cracks in Scientific Explanations Are Real.

Some of physics’ difficulty belongs to nature. Some appears when explanations hide the physical steps between a source, the signal that carries evidence of it, the receiver and the mechanism used to explain what is measured.

Between the Beats follows those steps through fiction — from cosmological signals and moving observers to electromagnetic fields and organised matter.

The measurements remain. The mechanisms used to explain them are examined again.

No equations. Not even E=mc2. The novels remain novels. The technical arguments appear only in the companion, when you choose to go deeper.

Why this exists

The questions began before the books.

A childhood fascination with stars and planets became a lifelong discomfort with small jumps in familiar explanations. Engineering made the questions clearer; fiction finally gave them a way out.

The trilogy

One story, a changing physical question

The books keep the measurements in place while each moves the unresolved physical step somewhere new: into the journey of the signal, the moving receiver, or the organised matter that emits and receives light.

Novel One

Between the Beats

Publication scheduled · September 3, 2026

The first book explores cosmology through the propagation of light. A telescope receives an electromagnetic signal only after an immense journey. What if that journey has physical transfer properties that belong inside the reconstruction of cosmic history?

Central question: Did we infer the cosmos before we understood the channel?

Novel Two

Speed Without a Message

Complete manuscript · planned autumn 2026

The second book separates the speed of a travelling light front from the rate at which it encounters a moving receiver, then follows that distinction through Doppler reception, clock synchronization and Lorentz transformations.

Central question: What actually moves at c — and what does not have to?

Read an excerpt

Novel Three

Titraj

Complete manuscript · planned early 2027

The third book turns from propagation to mechanism. It asks whether emission, absorption and electron escape can be reconstructed as responses of organised charged matter connected by an electromagnetic field, while keeping the successful quantum laws as quantitative obligations.

Central question: Can a physical mechanism be rebuilt beneath the formalism?

The question between the books and the physics

What physical step sits between what was measured and the mechanism used to explain it?
Source→Signal→Receiver→Measurement→Mechanism

The portal does not ask you to accept a finished replacement theory. It asks you to keep these stages separate long enough to inspect what is measured, what is reconstructed and what still has to be explained physically.

July 18, 2026 · Development note

The second passenger in the train

The familiar train argument focuses on the passenger at the midpoint. Adding another passenger reveals something more basic: simultaneous reception belongs to the local meeting place of the two flashes, not automatically to an entire reference frame.
