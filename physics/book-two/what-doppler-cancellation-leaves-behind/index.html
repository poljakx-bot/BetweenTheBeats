When a Clock Cancels Motion, What Remains?

Atomic clocks are physical devices. Some clock architectures deliberately suppress first-order Doppler sensitivity. In an ideal two-direction encounter model the odd term cancels, yet the reciprocal encounter intervals already contain the exact denominator that appears in the Lorentz factor.

Companion essay

Phenomenon → Prediction → Test

Cancellation removes direction, not automatically every dependence on motion.

The key question is not whether a \(v^2/c^2\) term can appear — it can. The scientific question is which symmetric combination a real clock physically realises, and whether different clock constructions leave the same residual response after their known systematics are removed.

Intuition

Two directions give opposite first-order Doppler terms

Take a wave of frequency \(f_0\) and wavelength \(\lambda_0=c/f_0\) in one chosen external frame. A receiver moving at speed \(v\) meets one propagation direction more slowly and the opposite direction more quickly.

\[f_-=f_0(1-\beta),\qquad f_+=f_0(1+\beta),\qquad \beta=\frac{v}{c}.\]

The arithmetic mean is

\[\frac{f_++f_-}{2}=f_0.\]

So a simple symmetric average of the two frequencies removes the first-order Doppler term exactly.

This is important: first-order cancellation by itself does not produce the Lorentz factor.

The reciprocal intervals contain more structure

The corresponding encounter periods are

\[T_-=\frac{T_0}{1-\beta},\qquad T_+=\frac{T_0}{1+\beta}.\]

Multiplying the two directions gives

\[T_+T_-=\frac{T_0^2}{1-\beta^2}.\]

Taking their geometric mean gives

\[\boxed{\sqrt{T_+T_-}=\frac{T_0}{\sqrt{1-\beta^2}}=\gamma T_0}.\]

Equivalently,

\[\boxed{\sqrt{f_+f_-}=f_0\sqrt{1-\beta^2}=\frac{f_0}{\gamma}}.\]

What has and has not been shown

No relativistic Doppler formula was inserted into those equations. The Lorentz-shaped factor emerged from the reciprocal two-direction encounter intervals.

But this is still a mathematical identity, not yet a theory of an atomic clock. A real clock must tell us whether its phase accumulation, resonance condition or feedback loop physically corresponds to that geometric combination, to an arithmetic combination, or to something more complicated.

Why atomic clocks make the question physical

Cold-atom clocks really do control Doppler-sensitive biases, and fountain clocks send the same atomic cloud through a microwave cavity on the way up and again on the way down. The direction of atomic velocity reverses; gravity does not.

That makes the architecture itself part of the physical experiment.

The Argument

Begin without the Lorentz factor

Let the external-frame wave satisfy

\[\lambda_0=\frac{c}{f_0},\qquad T_0=\frac{1}{f_0}.\]

For motion parallel to one travelling wave, successive fronts close on the receiver at \(c-v\). The encounter frequency is therefore

\[f_-=\frac{c-v}{\lambda_0}=f_0(1-\beta).\]

For the opposite propagation direction the closing rate is \(c+v\), giving

\[f_+=\frac{c+v}{\lambda_0}=f_0(1+\beta).\]

These are encounter-frequency relations in one chosen frame. No time-dilation factor has been assumed.

Odd terms cancel in frequency

\[\frac{f_++f_-}{2}=\frac{f_0[(1+\beta)+(1-\beta)]}{2}=f_0.\]

This is the clean first-order cancellation. It also shows why it would be wrong to claim that arithmetic Doppler cancellation itself leaves a \(\gamma\) residual.

Invert before combining

Because period is the reciprocal of frequency,

\[T_+=\frac{1}{f_+}=\frac{T_0}{1+\beta},\qquad T_-=\frac{T_0}{1-\beta}.\]

The product is

\[T_+T_-=\frac{T_0^2}{(1+\beta)(1-\beta)}=\frac{T_0^2}{1-\beta^2}.\]

Therefore

\[\boxed{\frac{\sqrt{T_+T_-}}{T_0}=\frac{1}{\sqrt{1-\beta^2}}\equiv\gamma}.\]

The same identity written in frequency form is

\[\boxed{\frac{\sqrt{f_+f_-}}{f_0}=\sqrt{1-\beta^2}=\frac{1}{\gamma}}.\]

The low-speed term is already quadratic

Expanding,

\[\gamma=1+\frac{1}{2}\beta^2+\frac{3}{8}\beta^4+\cdots\]

and

\[\sqrt{1-\beta^2}=1-\frac{1}{2}\beta^2-\frac{1}{8}\beta^4-\cdots.\]

Once the direction-changing odd term has disappeared, the leading surviving structure is quadratic in velocity.

Not every symmetric combination gives the same answer

The arithmetic mean of the two periods is

\[\frac{T_++T_-}{2}=\frac{T_0}{1-\beta^2}=\gamma^2T_0.\]

The harmonic mean returns \(T_0\). The geometric mean returns \(\gamma T_0\).

The appearance of \(1-\beta^2\) is forced by the two reciprocal directions. The square root that produces exactly \(\gamma\) is a further choice of how the two directions are physically combined.

That is the experimental question

A clock is not an abstract symbol that automatically chooses a mathematical mean. It has a physical interrogation sequence, cavity field, atomic trajectory, phase evolution, state-selection process and servo rule.

The mechanism must therefore determine the functional

\[F(T_+,T_-,\text{phase},\text{trajectory},\ldots)\]

that becomes the reported clock frequency.

Fountain clocks provide a natural two-direction case

In a cesium fountain, atoms pass the microwave cavity going upward and then again after gravity reverses their vertical velocity. In an idealised symmetric description, contributions odd in the vertical velocity can reverse sign between the two passages, while even terms cannot be removed by that sign reversal alone.

Real fountains are more complicated: distributed cavity phase, atomic trajectories, launch geometry and other systematics have to be modelled. That complexity is not an objection to the question — it is the mechanism through which the question must be answered.

From phenomenon to prediction

General relativity treats the gravitational redshift and proper-time effect as universal after clock-specific systematics are removed. A mechanism-centred alternative permits a sharper possibility:

\[\boxed{\text{different physical clock architectures may leave different residual responses to }v\text{ and }U.}\]

A convenient parameterisation is

\[\frac{d\ln\nu_i}{dU}=\frac{1+\alpha_i}{c^2}.\]

Universality requires \(\alpha_i=0\) for ideal clocks. A construction-dependent mechanism would permit \(\alpha_i\neq\alpha_j\), subject to existing experimental bounds.

What Hafele–Keating contributes

The original around-the-world experiment flew four cesium beam clocks. Their individual accumulated readings were not identical; the published relativistic result was obtained from the ensemble trend and its scatter.

That scatter is not evidence for construction dependence: the four devices were nominally the same clock type and ordinary clock instability/systematics are sufficient to explain device-to-device differences. Its value here is methodological: real clocks are physical oscillators with individual histories, not abstract identical proper-time counters.

The cleaner test

A discriminating experiment would compare several specimens of several fundamentally different clock architectures under the same controlled changes of velocity and gravitational potential:

\[A_1,A_2,A_3\qquad B_1,B_2,B_3\qquad C_1,C_2,C_3.\]

Within-family scatter estimates device instability. Differences between the family means test architecture dependence.

Deep Notes

The central derivation on this page is deliberately non-circular. If one begins with the relativistic longitudinal Doppler formula, which already contains \(\gamma\), and later recovers a Lorentz factor by combining opposite directions, nothing has been explained. Here the starting point is narrower: in one chosen external frame the wave propagates at \(c\), the receiver moves at \(v\), and encounter cadence follows from the rate at which successive fronts meet that moving receiver.

That construction is not presented as a replacement for special relativity. It is used to expose a mathematical fact: reciprocal two-direction encounter intervals contain the factor \((1-\beta^2)^{-1}\) before any Lorentz time transformation has been invoked.

The remaining scientific burden is to show what combination a real clock physically measures.

Step 1 — encounter frequencies

With \(\lambda_0=c/f_0\),

\[f_-=f_0(1-\beta),\qquad f_+=f_0(1+\beta).\]

The labels \(+\) and \(-\) refer only to the two opposite encounter geometries.

Step 2 — direct frequency cancellation

\[\bar f_{arith}=\frac{f_++f_-}{2}=f_0.\]

There is no hidden second-order remainder in this arithmetic mean. This prevents the argument from claiming more than the algebra gives.

Step 3 — reciprocal intervals

\[T_+=\frac{T_0}{1+\beta},\qquad T_-=\frac{T_0}{1-\beta}.\]

Now the opposite signs occupy denominators. Their symmetric product is

\[T_+T_-=\frac{T_0^2}{1-\beta^2}.\]

Two immediate Lorentz-shaped identities follow:

\[\boxed{\sqrt{T_+T_-}=\gamma T_0},\qquad \boxed{\sqrt{f_+f_-}=\frac{f_0}{\gamma}}.\]

Why this resembles time dilation so closely

In standard SR the rate of a moving ideal clock relative to coordinate time carries the same factor

\[\frac{f(v)}{f_0}=\sqrt{1-\beta^2}=\frac{1}{\gamma}.\]

The encounter construction has therefore generated the identical algebraic function.

But equality of functions is not equality of mechanisms. To identify the two physically, one must derive why the clock's reported rate is the geometric mean of the two reciprocal directional responses, or derive an equivalent phase rule from the actual apparatus.

Why the geometric mean cannot simply be chosen because it works

Several symmetric means are possible:

\[\begin{aligned}\text{frequency arithmetic mean}&=f_0,\\\text{period arithmetic mean}&=\gamma^2T_0,\\\text{period geometric mean}&=\gamma T_0,\\\text{period harmonic mean}&=T_0.\end{aligned}\]

The correct observable must come from the clock dynamics, not from selecting the expression that resembles a desired theory.

What real Doppler cancellation demonstrates

Cold-atom clock work explicitly evaluates and suppresses first-order Doppler frequency biases. That establishes an experimentally grounded point: motion relative to the interrogating field can shift the clock output unless the apparatus is designed or adjusted to cancel it.

For fountain clocks, NIST describes the atomic cloud passing the microwave cavity twice, once upward and once downward. NIST fountain work also treats distributed cavity phase as a trajectory-dependent frequency bias. Thus direction, phase sampling and physical geometry are already part of the metrological model.

Gravity in the fountain

Gravity does not change sign between the two cavity passages. It changes the atomic velocity continuously:

\[v(t)=v_0-gt.\]

For an idealised pair of cavity crossings at equal speed magnitude,

\[v_{up}=+|v_c|,\qquad v_{down}=-|v_c|.\]

An effect proportional to \(v\) reverses sign. An effect proportional to \(v^2\) does not. Gravity determines when the reversal occurs, the crossing velocities, the flight time and the trajectory through the field.

This does not show that gravitational redshift is a Doppler artefact. It shows why an alternative mechanism that assigns part of the clock response to its physical trajectory has a concrete calculation to perform.

Existing universality tests constrain the proposal

Different atomic standards have already been compared while gravitational potential varied. Long-term \(^{87}\mathrm{Rb}/^{133}\mathrm{Cs}\) fountain comparisons place strong bounds on any differential gravitational response between those transition standards.

That matters: a construction-dependent hypothesis is not free to predict large arbitrary differences. It must fit inside existing comparison limits and then identify a cleaner architecture contrast that has not already been excluded.

A stronger architecture test

Changing atomic species and changing clock construction at the same time mixes two questions. The cleaner experiment would hold as much atomic physics fixed as possible while changing interrogation geometry or clock architecture, or would use several specimens of each architecture so that device scatter can be separated from construction-level response.

Write the measured fractional shift schematically as

\[\delta_i(v,U)=\delta_{universal}(v,U)+r_i(v,U).\]

Standard universality requires

\[r_i-r_j=0\]

within known systematic uncertainties. The alternative prediction is a reproducible residual grouped by architecture rather than by individual device.

The exact claim

\[\boxed{\text{two-direction cancellation exposes an even-in-velocity structure; it does not by itself identify that structure with time dilation}.}\]

The fact that the geometric mean reproduces the Lorentz factor is the phenomenon. Deriving the clock's combination rule is the mechanism. Comparing different constructions is the test.
