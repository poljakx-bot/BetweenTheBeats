Physics Path III · Developed alongside Titraj

Electromagnetic Reception in Matter

These themes do not present a finished alternative physics. They follow a sequence of questions: what is actually observed, what the successful formalism establishes, and how far we are entitled to go when imagining a physical mechanism beneath it.

EM field→Organised matter→Response→Local output

The aim is to expose assumptions and open a physical possibility, not to pretend that the possibility has already become a complete theory.

Themes

Six questions, three reading depths each

Read them in sequence or enter through the question that interests you.

A working physical intuition, its strongest speculation, and the line beyond which the portal deliberately stops.
