The search

Questions that never went away

My search began when I was a boy.

I loved physics and stories about how stars, planets and the universe came to be. I wanted to understand not only what we could observe, but how those observations led to the explanations we accepted.

As I grew older, I began to notice small gaps in some of those explanations—places where the logical thread seemed to jump before reaching its conclusion.

I could sense the questions, but I could not yet formulate them clearly.

Books on popular physics gave me more knowledge, but they did not close those gaps. Later, while studying electrical engineering and telecommunications, I gained a stronger foundation in signals, waves and measurement. The questions became more precise, but they did not disappear.

They remained in the background for years.

Eventually, they began to find their way out through fiction.

What started as one novel became three, because one story could not contain all of the questions.

The books and the questions

The measurements remain. The missing physical step moves.

The trilogy does not ask the reader to reject established measurements. Each book asks whether the mechanism assigned beneath them is physically complete enough for the conclusion being drawn.

Book One

The signal and its journey

Between the Beats begins with astronomy as a received-signal problem. If light reaches us only after crossing an immense physical path, what did that journey do to the signal before we used it to reconstruct cosmic history?

Did we infer the cosmos before we understood the channel?

Book Two

Propagation, encounter and observation

Speed Without a Message separates the propagation speed of light from the rate at which a moving receiver encounters it, then follows that distinction into Doppler reception, synchronization and Lorentz transformations.

What actually moves at c — and what does not have to?

Book Three

Reconstructing the mechanism

Titraj turns to the material boundaries. It asks whether emission, absorption and local electron output can be connected through a more explicit mechanism of electromagnetic fields acting on organised charged matter.

Why a portal beside the fiction?

The questions grew beyond the space inside the novels.

The novels keep the measurements in place and keep equations outside the story. The companion exists because the physical questions eventually needed more room than fiction should give them.

Here, a measured outcome can be separated from the mathematical description that predicts it and from the physical mechanism proposed beneath that mathematics.

You can enter through the characters and their lives.

You can begin with a simple question.

Or you can go directly to the derivations, sources, objections and unresolved experimental obligations.

Method and transparency

AI assisted the process, not the authorship decisions.

AI tools were used during development to challenge arguments, compare formulations, organise drafts and assist revision. The questions, interpretations, editorial decisions and responsibility for the final text remain the author’s.

AI assistance is not presented as a substitute for specialist review; the portal makes the reasoning, evidence and remaining gaps open to examination.

What you have found here is not a finished new physics.

It is a public attempt to keep the physical steps visible long enough to ask whether a different mechanism can eventually survive the same measurements.

The author’s starting point

Background, authorship and limits

Engineering background

Ned Fielder is the pen name of Nenad Poljak, an electrical engineer educated at the University of Belgrade in telecommunications, with a particular interest in signal processing. That background shaped the insistence on separating source, propagation, receiver, measurement and reconstruction.

An open argument, not a finished theory

Engineering training, fiction and AI assistance are not substitutes for specialist peer review. The portal makes the questions public so that the reasoning, evidence and remaining gaps can be examined.
